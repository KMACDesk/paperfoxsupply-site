Low-Poly Fox 3D Print Pack

A digital download of print-ready files for a home FDM 3D printer, made for the Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (single-colour STL files for any printer, plus multi-colour 3MF files in our orange, white and navy for printers with an AMS): a curled-up table fox (about 80 mm), a fox planter with a drainage hole and its own saucer, fox earrings (studs and dangles, about 25 mm and light), and a fox pen and pencil holder. Every model prints without supports with a flat bottom (the earrings weigh about 1 g each), and was checked by a computer for watertight geometry, overhangs and wall thickness. Includes a print guide with layer height, infill and orientation. You need your own 3D printer; nothing is shipped. Designed with AI assistance; the models are generated by code. Personal use: please do not resell or share the files.
